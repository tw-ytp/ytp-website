## About

The Young Turing Program (YTP), named after Alan Turing, the father of computer science, is a social good initiative of SYSTEX Corporation, running in Taiwan since 2016. Junior-high and senior-high students form teams of three, take an online preliminary round, then a one-day on-site algorithm contest in Taipei. Top senior-high teams go on to a mentored project stage with university professors and industry volunteers, a hackathon, and a selection for an overseas study visit to Silicon Valley companies and university labs.

## International program (iYTP)

YTP has previously run an international edition for senior-high students in the Asia-Pacific region (registration on the old site last updated for the 2023 season, with a coding competition in September and a project challenge and free visit to Taiwan the following year).
